Loyal guardianship and care for the pets you love.

Petriotic — Pet + Patriot — is a pet-care and lost-pet app for Android and iOS, launching in South Africa.

Coming soon to Google Play and the App Store.

What Petriotic is for

Everyday care, and a clear plan if a pet goes missing — in one place.

  • Pet profiles and a private health book

    Register each pet once. Keep vaccination and health records in a health book that stays private, and set reminders for upcoming vaccination dates.

  • Lost-pet posters and private sightings

    If a pet goes missing, owners review and approve what is made public and can create a poster. Sightings sent in by others reach the owner privately.

  • A local pet services directory

    Find pet services near you by category and area, and open directions in your maps app.

Good to know

Petriotic is a tool for owners. It does not promise that a lost pet will be found, track pets by GPS, dispatch rescue, give medical diagnoses or guarantee how far a poster is seen. It is not part of any government, emergency service or veterinary authority.

How we handle personal information is set out on the privacy page.
