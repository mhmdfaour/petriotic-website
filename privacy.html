Privacy

The principles Petriotic is being built on, ahead of its launch in South Africa.

Pre-launch draft — the full privacy notice will be published before the app launches and is subject to legal review.

Our privacy principles

  • Private by default. Pet profiles and health books are private by default.
  • Owners decide what is public. If a pet is reported lost, only the details the owner has approved are made public.
  • No selling of health data. We do not sell health data.
  • Your requests. You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal information using the contact details below.
  • South African law. We will process personal information in line with South Africa’s Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).
